# Mission: Technical Mandarin for AIOps Engineering

## Why
Re-activate dormant HSK 3 Mandarin and build domain-specific technical fluency to communicate directly and credibly with a Chinese engineering superior at Huawei. The goal is to move from rusted, general vocabulary to clear spoken technical briefings on AIOps pipelines, incident root-cause analysis (RCA), anomaly detection, and system reliability.

## Success looks like
- Deliver a concise, natural 60-second spoken incident or status briefing (故障汇报) to a Chinese manager using professional cause-and-effect framing (`由于...导致...`, `初步判断...`, `目前已经...`).
- Discuss core AIOps architecture and workflows in Mandarin: observability telemetry (`指标` metrics, `日志` logs, `调用链` traces), alert noise reduction (`告警降噪`, `告警风暴`, `误报/漏报`), fault localization (`故障定位`), and root cause analysis (`根因分析`).
- Explain model and operational trade-offs during standups or 1-on-1s: threshold tuning (`阈值调整`), anomaly detection (`异常检测`), rollbacks (`回滚`), latency bottlenecks (`延迟瓶颈`), and postmortems (`复盘`).
- Understand rapid spoken follow-up questions from a Chinese tech lead (`根本原因是什么？`, `什么时候能恢复？`, `准确率怎么样？`) and answer without falling back to English sentence skeletons.

## Constraints
- Passed HSK 3 roughly 7 years ago: core Mandarin sentence structure (SVO, time-before-place, `把/被`, `因为...所以...`) is dormant in storage memory, requiring retrieval reactivation rather than beginner grammar drills.
- Speaking to a technical manager requires high-signal workplace phrasing; lessons must always pair Simplified Chinese characters (汉字) with Pinyin tones so reading rust never blocks spoken practice.
- Busy full-time engineering schedule: lessons must be tightly scoped (10–15 minutes) and immediately usable in real work conversations at Huawei.

## Out of scope
- Literary Chinese, classical idioms (`成语`), or handwriting characters from memory.
- Beginner HSK 1–2 travel/lifestyle survival topics (ordering food, shopping, family hobbies).
- Non-technical business Mandarin (legal contracts, accounting, marketing copy).
