## Evidence
User explicitly stated: *"I want to relearn mandarin, I was good enough to pass at least HSK 3 like 7 years ago but have gotten worse due to unuse. I want to at least be able to speak technically to my chinese superior technically. I work at Huawei as an aiops engineer."*

## Implications
- **Do not re-teach beginner grammar from scratch**: Instead, reactivate existing HSK 3 grammar skeletons by slotting high-frequency AIOps nouns and verbs into them.
- **Bridge HSK 3 roots to technical compounds**: Show how familiar HSK 3 characters (`因` from `因为`, `报` from `报告/报纸`, `流` from `交流`, `正` from `正常`) combine into technical terms (`根因` root cause, `误报` false positive, `流量` traffic, `正常` normal/healthy).
- **Prioritize spoken workplace briefings (`汇报`)**: Focus early lessons on high-leverage manager interactions—incident status updates, root cause explanations, and model/alerting metric discussions—paired with Pinyin tone guides and interactive audio playback.
