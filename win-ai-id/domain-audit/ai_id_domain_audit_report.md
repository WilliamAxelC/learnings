## How to Register .ai.id Domains

1. **Registry Authority**: Managed by **PANDI** (Pengelola Nama Domain Internet Indonesia).
2. **General Availability Launch Date**: **October 5, 2026** (open to public registration).
3. **Where to Buy**:
   - **Niagahoster / PT Dewabisnis Digital Indonesia**: [niagahoster.co.id](https://niagahoster.co.id)
   - **IDCloudHost**: [idcloudhost.com](https://idcloudhost.com)
   - **Exabytes Indonesia**: [exabytes.co.id](https://exabytes.co.id)
   - **DomaiNesia**: [domainesia.com](https://domainesia.com)
   - **PANDI Direct**: [pandi.id](https://pandi.id) / [domain.id](https://domain.id)
4. **Documentation**: Standard Indonesian identity (KTP / Passport) or business registration (NIB/SIUP), significantly simpler and less restrictive than `.co.id`.
