# Module 04 Exercises: Production & Editing

Complete these hands-on drills to calibrate your recording space and editing workflow.

## Drill 1: The Audio Benchmark Test

Record a 30-second audio clip speaking naturally into your current setup.

1. **Noise Floor Test**: Stop talking for 5 seconds and record ambient room silence.
   - What level does the silence meter at in your DAW / editing software? `[Target: below -45 dB]`
   - Is there audible hum from computer fans, refrigerator, or air conditioning? `[Yes / No]`
   - Remediation applied (e.g. turned off AC, hung blanket): `[Describe action]`

2. **Vocal Gain Staging**: Speak at normal conversational volume:
   - Peak level: `[Target: -12 dB to -6 dB]`
   - Did any shout or laugh peak into 0 dB red clipping? `[Yes / No]`

3. **Processing Chain Log**: Apply these audio effects and note the difference:
   - High-Pass Filter cutoff: `[e.g., 80 Hz]`
   - Speech isolation / De-noise strength: `[e.g., 20%]`
   - Compressor threshold / ratio: `[e.g., Threshold -14 dB, Ratio 3:1]`
   - Final Integrated Loudness: `[Target: -14 LUFS]`

## Drill 2: 60-Second Assembly Cut

Record the vertical script you wrote in Module 03. Import it into your editor of choice (DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro, CapCut, or Final Cut).

- **Raw Footage Duration**: `[e.g., 1 minute 45 seconds]`
- **Post-Trim Duration**: `[Target: 45 to 55 seconds]`
- **Silence Elimination Check**: Confirm no gaps between sentences exceed 0.3 seconds.
- **Pattern Interrupts Added**:
  - Punch-in cut #1 at timestamp: `[e.g., 0:08]`
  - B-roll or screen recording overlay at timestamp: `[e.g., 0:22]`
  - Sound effect (whoosh/pop) timestamp: `[e.g., 0:14]`
- **Dialogue vs. Music Level**: Dialogue at -14 LUFS, Music bed at `[e.g., -24 dB]`.
