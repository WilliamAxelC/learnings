## 1. Executive Summary & Strategic Pivot

### The Transition from Promo to Evergreen Commercial Collateral
A grand opening promotion ("Cuci Gratis Bayar Pakai Doa") serves one primary function: **initial foot-traffic surge and barrier-to-trial elimination**. However, once the promotional window concludes, marketing collateral must pivot from *event-based novelty* to **sustainable value proposition and transparent price-to-value architecture**.

If a brochure continues displaying expired promos, it damages brand authority and breeds customer distrust. Conversely, if a post-promo brochure simply presents a dry, sterile price table, conversion plummets. 

The primary challenge of this redesign is:
> **How do we make the pricelist the focal hero without looking like an intimidating bill or a boring accounting spreadsheet?**

The answer lies in **Value-Anchored Pricelist Design**, **Visual Hierarchy Psychology**, and **Frictionless Local WhatsApp Conversion**.

## 2. Theoretical Principles of High-Converting Pricelist Architecture

### 2.1 Daniel Kahneman's Anchoring & Framing Heuristics
When consumers evaluate prices in isolation, they experience uncertainty. In behavioral economics, price evaluation is always comparative:
1. **Compromise Effect (Simonson, 1989):** When presented with three distinct tiers, consumers naturally gravitate toward the middle tier (the "compromise") or weigh the extreme tiers against their perceived lifestyle effort.
   - **Tier 1: Drop Off (Mulai Rp 4.000 / kg)**: The accessible volume anchor. Establishes Kimmy Laundry as affordable and economical for everyday college students (anak kos) and families.
   - **Tier 2: Full Service (Mulai Rp 8.000 / kg)**: The premium convenience tier (Cuci + Kering + Setrika Uap Wangi). Anchored against the customer's lost time—spending 3 hours ironing at home is worth far more than Rp 8.000/kg.
   - **Tier 3: Self Service (Mulai Rp 10.000 / load s/d 7 kg)**: The speed, privacy, and hygiene tier (30 Menit Selesai). Framing 7 kg for Rp 10.000 reveals an effective price of **hanya Rp 1.428 / kg**, making it the ultimate budget lifehack for tech-savvy/modern customers.
2. **Transparent Unit Clarification:** The fatal flaw of laundry flyers is confusing `/load` with `/kg`. Modern brochures must explicitly state the unit and capacity:
   - *Self Service:* Rp 10.000 / load (Kapasitas s/d 7 Kg)
   - *Drop Off:* Rp 4.000 / kg (Cuci Kering Lipat Rapi)
   - *Full Service:* Rp 8.000 / kg (Cuci Kering Setrika Uap Wangi Mewah)

### 2.2 Mental Accounting & Risk Mitigation (Richard Thaler & Amos Tversky)
In urban Indonesian residential areas (dense kos-kosan and family clusters around Tanjung Duren), laundry outsourcing carries three critical psychological pain points:
1. **Fear of Garment Mixing (*Takut Dicampur*):** Customers worry their clothes will be washed with someone else's dirty underwear or dirty rags.
   - *Copy Solution:* Prominent guarantee: **"1 Mesin 1 Pelanggan • 100% Tidak Pernah Dicampur"**.
2. **Fear of Fabric Damage & Chemical Odors (*Takut Apek / Rusak*):** Cheap detergents cause color fading or musty smells.
   - *Copy Solution:* **"Deterjen Premium & Pewangi Tahan Lama • Setrika Uap Anti Gosong"**.
3. **Logistical Friction (*Malas Bawa Cucian Berat*):** Carrying 10 kg of laundry on a motorbike in Jakarta heat is exhausting.
   - *Copy Solution:* **"GRATIS ANTAR JEMPUT RADIUS 5 KM"** featured prominently as a primary conversion catalyst.
