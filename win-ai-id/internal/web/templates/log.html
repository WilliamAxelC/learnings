Established the baseline philosophy: wins must feel friction-free. Auto-materializing scheduled habits, single-tap checks with haptic feedback, and a 4:00 AM local day rollover so late-night wins count properly.

  • Global Streak + Free Weekly Skip: Missing one day doesn't destroy motivation. One free skip per ISO week automatically keeps momentum alive.
